Research

This page brings together my research interests, previous academic work, and publications. My interests center on understanding machine learning models and their behavior, while my previous research has primarily focused on applied machine learning, particularly signal analysis. I hope to have many more interesting projects and experiences to share here in the future.

Current interests

Explainable AI & Explanation Faithfulness

Investigating how model predictions can be explained and whether these explanations faithfully reflect the actual decision-making process.

Mechanistic Interpretability & Internal Representations

Understanding how neural networks represent information and which internal mechanisms and computations give rise to their behavior.

Model Evaluation & Behavioral Analysis

Designing targeted experiments to uncover surprising capabilities, systematic weaknesses, and unexpected behaviors of AI models.

Knowledge, Reasoning & Factuality

Investigating how models acquire and use knowledge, perform reasoning, and why they sometimes produce incorrect or hallucinated information.

Generalization, Memorization & Robustness

Understanding what models truly learn, how they generalize to new situations, and when they rely on memorization or shortcuts.

Causal Inference & Effect Estimation

Investigating causal relationships through experiments and observational data, with a focus on identifying and estimating causal effects.
